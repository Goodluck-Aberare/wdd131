True Light Data Analytics

Site Name

True Light Data Analytics — This name reflects the mission to bring clarity and insight through data. It’s professional, memorable, and aligns with the brand’s values of transparency and intelligence.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to showcase data analytics services, including data visualization, predictive modeling, and business intelligence. It will also provide educational content, case studies, and a contact form for potential clients to request consultations or demos.

Scenarios

  • How can True Light help my business make better data-driven decisions?
  • What types of analytics services does True Light offer, and how do I request a consultation?

Color Schema

  • Midnight Blue (#003366): Used for headers, navigation bar, and footer background.
  • Sky Blue (#00BFFF): Used for buttons, links, and call-to-action highlights.
  • Light Gray (#F5F5F5): Used for section backgrounds and contrast.

Typography

  • Montserrat (sans-serif): Used for all headings and titles.
  • Open Sans (sans-serif): Used for body text and paragraphs.
  • Lora (serif): Used for quotes and callouts.
